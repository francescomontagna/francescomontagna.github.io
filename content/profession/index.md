Hey, nice to meet you! I'm Francesco, a (very happy to be!) researcher in **causality and AI for science**.

I work on theoretical and algorithmic causal discovery and causal representation learning, and on their application to understanding and simulating how cells behave.

I earned my PhD at the [University of Genoa](https://malga.unige.it/) in the ELLIS PhD program, supervised by [Lorenzo Rosasco](http://web.mit.edu/lrosasco/www/) and Francesco Locatello. Along the way I was a research intern at Amazon AWS in Tübingen and a visiting researcher in the CLeaR group at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/kunz1/), hosted by Kun Zhang.

I'm always happy to chat about collaborations and ideas — get in touch!
